From Debit and Credit to Creo

How the public tape anchors the generative unit of account. November 2026 · Hedera HCS $0.0008 per message.

Debit records what is owed (retrospective material custody). Credit records what is lent (prospective reputational belief). Creo originates verified value through human action without generating offsetting interest-bearing debt. The linguistic intersection of Latin creāre (“bring forth”) and Spanish creo (“I believe”) captures the requirement for collective cryptographic consensus.

Hederica.com is the Constitutive Tape: it anchors daily Merkle roots and attestation hashes onto Hedera Consensus Service under the governance of the Third Seat Charter. It establishes an immutable, auditable unit of account with constitutional due-process protections.

Fee economics and batching

2026 mainnet ConsensusSubmitMessage: $0.0008 USD flat. Direct timestamp already undercuts legacy card rails by >99% on micro-deeds. Client-side daily Merkle batching reduces effective cost further:

  • Direct: 0.016% on a $5 deed, 3–5 s deterministic aBFT finality.
  • 100 deeds/day: ~0.00016% effective.
  • 10,000 deeds/day: near-zero marginal notarization.

Only the minimal cryptographic hash required to prove a deed is written. Sensitive personal and health records remain strictly local on the user’s phone (lgme.app Switch).

Third Seat invariants (encoded in the Charter)

  • Seat Symmetry: Non-transferable personal seat standing beside state and firm under a single published rulebook. Unconditional right to decline any exchange at no cost; immunity from unpublished rules; 7-day peer-adjudicated challenge window (non-statutory holds automatically lapse).
  • Floor Seniority: Basic subsistence reserves maintain strict priority over speculative allocation or surplus distribution.
  • Data Minimization & Exit Sovereignty: Members may export their complete cryptographic history and exit at any time.

Phased roadmap (Tape role)

  1. Closed-circle local clearing (lgme.app) builds portable reputation without wallets.
  2. Daily Merkle roots published here at $0.0008 per anchor — immutable temporal proof.
  3. Cross-circle clearing: verified IDidIt receipts become redeemable across cooperatives.
  4. Federated one-member-one-vote cooperatives under ratified Third Seat bylaws.

The tape does not mint speculative tokens. It does not move money. It records the fact of verified work so that informal human care and civic labor can enter the balance sheet at near-zero marginal cost.
