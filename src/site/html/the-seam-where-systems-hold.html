What belongs on the tape

In the essay, two domains are set beside each other. Provenance is the discrete tape of attested work. Clearing is the mutual-credit space where neighbors settle without waiting for outside cash. The seam between them is the point of the argument.

Hederica is the constitutive tape in that picture: a public book for work that banks never counted. A timestamp, if one is ever written, would record a deed. It would not move money. No such write is made today.

The Third Seat is not a clerk on the chain

The source text describes the Third Seat as an adjunction between provenance and clearing, and as a witness that will not mint credit without a recorded deed and will not let the books drift from a zero-sum conservation rule. Read as design, that is a refusal of a hidden issuer and of a hidden judge.

On this site the Third Seat is not a referee who holds the book for someone else. The person keeps the book. A hold, if one is ever used, has to give a reason and can be challenged. That is charter language, not a live Hedera process.

Clearing without claiming a write

The Rafi, Ana, and Marcus story in the essay shows how a closed circle of favors can cancel without a bank dollar, while a record of the work can remain. An uneven circle would cancel only the shared part and leave a named remainder. Floor Seniority would keep groceries ahead of luxury claims. A two-generation sunset would refuse forever debts.

Those clearing and sunset rules are design aims. They are not running on Hedera, and this page does not treat a fee schedule or a mainnet message as an activity that has begun.
