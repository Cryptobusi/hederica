The Third Seat

Draft article, v1 (Nov 10, 2026). Not legal advice; pending counsel review.

The Third Seat is the person's seat. It stands beside the state and the firm, under one published rule, with equal standing.

The nonprofit is not an arbiter. It does not decide a member's legal rights.

  1. Definition. The Third Seat is the seat each member holds as an individual, beside the state and the firm, under one published set of rules. It is personal and cannot be delegated, sold, or held by any organization, including the nonprofit.
  2. Basis. By joining, members agree to extend to one another, and to require of the organization, the fair-process standards the Constitution requires of government: notice, a stated reason, a fair hearing, and the least restrictive means. This is a private agreement among members. It does not claim to enforce constitutional rights against anyone else.
  3. Right to decline. A member may decline any exchange at no cost or penalty. The refusal is recorded. Nothing already settled is undone.
  4. Published rules. No rule may be applied to a member unless it was published before it was used. An unpublished rule has no effect.
  5. Right to challenge. A member may challenge any hold, block, or veto that affects them. Randomly chosen members with no stake in the matter decide within 7 days. If they don't decide in time, the hold lapses, unless clause 7 applies.
  6. Equal standing. No rule gives the state, a firm, or the nonprofit a power over a member's exchange that the member does not hold in return, except as clause 7 allows.
  7. Holds required by law. The organization will comply with anti-money-laundering, sanctions, tax, and court-order obligations. Any such hold must (a) cite the law or order requiring it; (b) be recorded, with notice to the member where the law allows; (c) be no broader or longer than the law requires; and (d) be reviewed every 30 days and lifted when its legal basis ends. Nothing in this Article requires anyone to break the law.
  8. What the nonprofit is not. The nonprofit does not hold members' funds or keys, does not decide anyone's legal rights, and does not issue a currency. No hour, credit, or token is a promise of profit.
  9. Privacy. Only the smallest fact needed to prove a deed is recorded, and only with consent. Pseudonyms are allowed where the law allows.
  10. Exit. Membership is voluntary. A member may leave at any time and take a full copy of their records.
  11. Disputes. Members first try to settle a dispute between themselves, and the attempt is recorded. If that fails, the dispute goes to the courts of the state of incorporation.
  12. Amendment. This Article may be amended to widen members' protections, never to weaken clauses 3, 4, 5, or 10. An amendment that would weaken them is void.
  13. Severability. If any part of this Article is held invalid, the rest stays in force.

Short form (for headers, glossaries, cards):

The Third Seat is your seat, held by you alone, with equal standing beside the state and the firm. You may decline any exchange at no cost, no unpublished rule can bind you, and any hold on your exchange must give a reason and can be challenged, decided within 7 days or it lapses. Holds the law requires are cited, recorded, and kept no broader or longer than the law demands.
